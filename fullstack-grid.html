  • Irin Pervin
  • 10 Dec 2021

Careers at Sunrisemitra Global Technologies

Join our team of innovators and creators.

Full Stack Developer Intern

Location: Kallur, Khammam

Type:Intership -  3 Months

Description: We are seeking a motivated and eager Full Stack Developer Intern to join our development team. This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in both front-end and back-end web development, contribute to real projects, and learn from experienced professionals. As a Full Stack Developer Intern, you will be involved in building and maintaining web applications that provide meaningful solutions to our users.

Key Responsibilities::

  • Collaborate with the development team to design, develop, and deploy web applications with a focus on both front-end and back-end components.
  • Assist in the creation of responsive and user-friendly web interfaces using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frameworks.
  • Participate in the development and integration of server-side logic, databases, and APIs using languages such as Python, Node.js, Ruby, or others.
  • Contribute to the entire development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, and deployment.
  • Work closely with UX/UI designers to ensure seamless integration of design and functionality.
  • Write clean, well-documented code and adhere to coding best practices and standards.
  • Collaborate with other team members to troubleshoot, debug, and resolve technical issues as they arise.
  • Keep up-to-date with industry trends and emerging technologies to suggest innovative ideas and improvements for projects.
  • Assist in maintaining and updating existing web applications, implementing enhancements, and fixing bugs.
  • Participate in code reviews to ensure code quality, share knowledge, and learn from constructive feedback.
  • Take ownership of assigned tasks and projects, ensuring they are completed on time and meet the defined requirements.

    • Qualifications:

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or recently graduated).
  • Proficiency in front-end technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Familiarity with JavaScript frameworks/libraries such as React, Angular, or Vue.js.
  • Basic understanding of back-end development concepts and languages, such as Python, Node.js, Ruby, or Java.
  • Knowledge of databases and experience with SQL or NoSQL databases.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a passion for learning new technologies.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
  • Prior experience with version control systems (e.g., Git) is a plus.
  • A portfolio showcasing personal or academic projects is highly desirable.

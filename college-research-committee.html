College Research Committee

On 15/03/2024, the Research and Development unit proposed measures to strengthen academic and research output. Key points include financial support of ₹25,000 per annum for three years (₹75,000 total) for Ph.D. pursuing lecturers, repayable via salary deductions with a one-year mandatory service after completion. No aid will be given for simple participation in events, but faculty presenting papers at approved conferences may receive up to ₹2,000 along with three days of ‘on duty’ leave. Publishing in reputed UGC Care Group and indexed journals will receive ₹5,000 per paper, limited to two publications annually.

Faculty attending FDPs will get full ‘on duty’ leave for the programme duration, with registration fees borne personally. Each lecturer should publish two research papers and participate in at least eight academic activities annually, while each department should conduct a minimum of two programmes per year to encourage a strong research culture.

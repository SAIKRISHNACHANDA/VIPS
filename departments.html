B. PHARMACY

B Pharmacy (Bachelor of Pharmacy) commonly referred as B Pharm, started in 2006 at Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, is a 4-year (8 semesters) undergraduate programme that serves as the foundation for individuals aspiring to become pharmacists and contribute to the well-being of society.

It’s a comprehensive undergraduate course in pharmaceutical sciences that deeply explores the fascinating world of medicines. The programme focuses on theoretical learning and also encourages students to develop a practical understanding of how drugs are created, tested and prescribed safely.

Career Opportunities:

Roles in hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, regulatory agencies.

PHARM D

The Pharm.D (Doctor of Pharmacy) programme started in 2011 at Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, is a 6-year professional clinical doctorate designed to develop competent clinical pharmacists capable of providing evidence-based pharmaceutical care.

The curriculum is PCI-approved and integrates pharmaceutical sciences with advanced clinical training. Students undergo continuous clinical postings at Hospitals, gaining skills in patient counselling, medication therapy management, pharmacovigilance, and clinical decision-making.

The programme emphasizes therapeutics, biostatistics, research methodology, and interdisciplinary learning. A one-year rotational internship across specialties prepares graduates for careers in clinical pharmacy, drug safety, clinical research, hospital pharmacy, and digital health.

Career Opportunities:

Pharm. D graduates are highly sought after across clinical, research, regulatory, and pharmaceutical industries. They are equipped to take on specialized roles that bridge patient care, drug safety, and healthcare management.

  • Clinical Pharmacist
  • Pharmacovigilance Associate
  • Drug Safety Scientist
  • Clinical Research Associate
  • Clinical Data Manager
  • Medication Safety Officer
  • Drug Information Specialist
  • Medical Coding Specialist
  • Healthcare Analyst

PHARM D (PB) Post Baccalaureate

The Pharm.D (Post Baccalaureate) is a 3-year, started in 2022 at Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences is PCI-approved doctoral program designed for B.Pharm graduates to gain advanced clinical training.

As a lateral entry (4th year) course, it covers pharmacotherapy, research, and patient care, culminating in one year of internship/residency. It prepares graduates for roles as clinical pharmacists or pharmacovigilance associates.

M. PHARMACY

Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm), is a 2-year (4-semester) postgraduate program specializing in pharmaceutical sciences, focusing on drug development, manufacturing, and research.

Candidates need a B.Pharm degree with 50-55% marks and a valid GPAT score.

Our institution offers four different Master of Pharmacy courses:

M. Pharmacy - Pharmaceutics

M.Pharm in Pharmaceutics, started in 2010 at Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences is a 2-year postgraduate program focused on the science of dosage form design, drug delivery systems, and industrial manufacturing.

It prepares students to develop safe, effective, and stable medicines. The program emphasizes advanced manufacturing technologies, formulation research, and regulatory requirements.

M. Pharmacy - Pharmacology

M.Pharm in Pharmacology, started in 2010, focuses on advanced study of drug mechanisms, interactions, and therapeutic effects on biological systems.

It covers preclinical screening, molecular pharmacology, toxicology, and research including drug discovery.

M. Pharmacy - Industrial Pharmacy

M.Pharm in Industrial Pharmacy, started in 2014, focuses on drug development, manufacturing technology, formulation development, and regulatory compliance.

M. Pharmacy - Pharmaceutical Analysis

M.Pharm in Pharmaceutical Analysis, started in 2021, focuses on advanced techniques to identify, quantify, and purify drug substances.

It includes method development, validation, and instrumental analysis (HPLC, GC).

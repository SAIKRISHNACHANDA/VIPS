Formation of IQAC

The IQAC of Vaagdevi Degree & PG College, Hanamkonda, T.S. was formed on 7th July 2021. The cell is committed to planning, guiding, and monitoring the quality assurance, sustenance, and enhancement activities of the college. In line with the vision and mission of the college, IQAC develops quality policies and standardization measures for smooth functioning.

Vision

To make quality the defining aspect of higher education in India through internal and external quality assessment, promotion, and sustainability efforts. The IQAC seeks to be a catalyst in promoting academics, research, and extension activities, leading to creative solutions in tune with the mission of the college.

Mission

  • Enhance the quality of learning, teaching, and research in higher education.
  • Promote self-evaluation, accountability, and autonomy while fostering innovation.
  • Conduct research and training programmes on quality-related topics.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to promote quality assessment.

Genesis

Shaping Futures with Excellence

Established in 1993 by the Viswambhara Educational Society, Vaagdevi Degree & P.G. College in Hanamkonda has been a center of academic excellence and discipline. Affiliated with Kakatiya University, the institution expanded in 1995 by introducing P.G. courses to meet the educational aspirations of students. Strategically located in the heart of the tri-cities of Warangal, the college has grown into one of the most sought-after institutions, committed to nurturing both academic brilliance and holistic development.

  • Premier institution with a strong legacy since 1993
  • Successfully introduced P.G. courses in 1995
  • Centrally located in the tri-cities of Warangal
  • Focus on academic excellence and discipline
  • Proven track record of successful student placements
  • Commitment to overall student development
  • Excellence in academics, sports, and cultural activities

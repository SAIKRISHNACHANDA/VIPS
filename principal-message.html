Principal's Message

Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, established in 2006, is a premier institution dedicated to excellence in pharmaceutical education and research. The institute offers B. Pharm., M. Pharm., and Pharm. D programs, designed to meet the dynamic needs of the healthcare sector where pharmacists play a crucial role. Accredited by NAAC, affiliated to Kakatiya University and approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), provides a strong academic foundation supported by innovation and professional ethics. The institute’s focus areas include start-up incubation and business acceleration, innovation and product development, research commercialization, Industry collaboration and entrepreneurship development. With experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, advanced laboratories, rich libraries and strong industry linkages, Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences ensures academic excellence, research advancement and successful career outcomes. It is an institution where knowledge is nurtured, innovation is encouraged and achievement is recognized.

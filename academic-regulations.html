Academic Regulations

Students must follow discipline, punctuality, and a minimum of 75% attendance. Ragging, use of mobiles in class, indecent behavior, and loitering during college hours are strictly prohibited. Respect towards teachers, peers, and campus property is mandatory. Proper dress code, regular class participation, and productive use of leisure hours in the library are encouraged. Any misconduct, damage to property, or violation of rules will lead to penalties, suspension, or disciplinary action.

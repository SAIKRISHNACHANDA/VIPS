Rules For Admission

Candidates seeking admission to I year of B.Sc./B.Com./BBM course must have passed the intermediate examination conducted by the State Board of Intermediate Education, Telangana, or any other examination recognized by the Kakatiya University as equivalent there to. Candidates must have registered their names through Online-Web Options must be given for seats allotment (admission).

Application Form & Admission Guidelines

Copies of the following certificates should be enclosed with the application form at the time of admission:

  • Inter Memo of Marks (Original)
  • Transfer Certificate (Original)
  • Bonafide & Study Certificate (6th to Inter)
  • Any other certificate (Sports / PH / NCC / NSS / Ex-Serviceman)
  • Caste Certificate (If applicable)
  • 4 recent Passport size color photos
  • Copy of Aadhar Card
  • Bridge Course Certificate

Candidates seeking admission to I year science group should have obtained not less than 40% marks in aggregate in the qualifying examination in the corresponding optional subject. However, this condition does not apply to candidates belonging to the SC/ST categories. Candidates who have passed vocational courses seeking admission to the Science group must wait for confirmation from the University. Students who passed the qualifying examination in a medium other than English are eligible for English Medium if they secure a minimum of 40% marks in English at Intermediate level.

The list of selected candidates will be displayed as per the University schedule on the notice board. The college reserves the right to cancel admission at any stage if it is found against the rules and regulations of the University or the Government.

Reservations

Reservation of seats will be applicable as per Government & University norms:

  • SC – 15%
  • ST – 5%
  • BC – 25% (Group A: 7%, Group B: 10%, Group C: 1%, Group D: 7%)
  • NCC & NSS – 2%
  • PH – 3%
  • Games & Sports – 2%
  • Extra-curricular Activities – 1%
  • Ex-Servicemen – 3%

If seats are not filled under the reserved quota, they will be allotted to candidates from the general pool.

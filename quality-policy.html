Quality Policy

The quality policy of Vaagdevi Degree & PG College is derived from the vision of the college, which aims to achieve academic excellence by providing skill-enhancing and value-enriching education, which helps not only the growth of the individual student but also enables us to become a rich source for nation building by offering talented youth to society.

Our Institution strives hard to provide a perfect platform to meet world-class challenges by inculcating innovative learning methods with a unique diversified curriculum and by adopting a need-based approach in academics while giving priority to moral and ethics which shape the individual to be fit for the ever-changing global environment.

We work hard to empower women through all-rounded and integrated education and prepare them for excelling in leadership and functional roles at home, in society, organisations, and the country, enabling them to live up to their full potential.

In compliance with the UGC guidelines, which emphasize the necessity of an internal mechanism for sustenance, assurance, and enhancement of quality in education by Higher Education Institutions, this quality policy document is set by the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of the college.

This policy also acknowledges the significance of the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), which emphasizes that "students have a unique role to play in ensuring the quality of Higher Education Institutions." The college's quality policy upholds the guarantees of students' "right to quality education" and places a high value on the instruction that students "demand quality education and demonstrate their commitment to quality education by accepting their responsibilities."

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of the college framed this quality policy, and the college's Governing Body approved it and gave their unwavering support to it.

Objectives Of The Quality Policy

  • To support, foster, and uphold the institutional culture in line with the college's mission and vision.
  • To put in place and maintain a Quality Management System (QMS) that guarantees adherence to guidelines.
  • To establish conditions for raising the standard of instruction through professional development opportunities and programs.
  • To empower faculty and staff to refresh their knowledge periodically to match real-world demands.
  • To enhance the standard of instruction by making the best use of learning resources and evaluating activities regularly.
  • To offer assistance and a dynamic environment for teaching and learning that promotes student success.
  • To offer channels for feedback to enhance services.
  • To create and uphold a quality assurance system for ongoing improvement.
  • To create a management information system for efficient quality administration.
  • To develop and monitor benchmarks, standards, and targets for all activity areas.
  • To establish integrated planning and quality assurance cycles communicated throughout the organization.
  • To arrange training to increase employees' ability to sustain continuous improvement systems.
  • To uphold accountability and responsibility in all interactions with stakeholders.
  • Create a gym.

Policy Statement

This policy expresses the college's commitment to achieving excellence in education through continuous improvement and high performance standards, fostering quality in all stakeholders, and enabling students to realize their full potential.

Implementation Of Quality Policy

To fulfill the promises in the quality assurance policy statement, the college established the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) as the driving force for achieving excellence through continuous quality improvement.

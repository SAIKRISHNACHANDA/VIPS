RTI Statutory Declaration

Section 4 (1) (b) of the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 covers the statutory declaration of Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Bollikunta (V), Khila Warangal (M), Warangal - 506005, Telangana State.

Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences is recognized by statutory regulatory authorities such as the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi, Government of Telangana, and is affiliated to Kakatiya University, Warangal, Telangana.

The institute runs as per the policies and rules made by the Government of Telangana and the affiliated University from time to time. The institute comes under the “Self-Financed” category; therefore, financial transactions are audited by private auditors. The institute has different committees to maintain discipline and ensure smooth functioning within the campus. All committees and cells are formed as per the guidelines of statutory bodies.

Applications under the RTI Act along with a Postal Order or Demand Draft of Rs. 10.00 in favor of the Principal, Vaagdevi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Bollikunta (V), Khila Warangal (M), Warangal - 506005, Telangana State, may be sent to the Public Information Officer (PIO) or submitted directly in the office. All information about the college is open to the public and can be obtained by citizens of India.

Pharmacy is one of the fastest emerging and dynamic fields for bright and satisfying career prospects. Students in this field will have to work with health care specialists and medicine manufactures in order to cater to the needs of the society. Pharmacy are duly approved by the Pharmacy Council of India and aim at preparing learners for careers in the highly dynamic and rapidly growing field of Pharmacy. Pharmacy is a versatile, dynamic, growing, and increasingly diverse profession which from its traditional form has transformed into a hub for “Global Healthcare” and evolved as a multidisciplinary and multifaceted field in recent times Pharmacists today are health care professionals whose professional responsibilities require them to keep abreast of developments and advances in knowledge & technology related to manufacture & use of medicines, professional standard requirements, laws governing pharmacy, etc. After successful completion of these pharmacy courses, the students are fully eligible and competent for finding job openings in companies, industries, and institutions which are engaged in the pharmaceuticals and healthcare fields, particularly at the entry to assistant level positions. Training and guidance are also offered by this faculty for professional and career development.

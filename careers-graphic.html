Careers at Sunrisemitra Global Technologies

Join our team of innovators and creators.

Graphic Designer.

Location: Kallur, Khammam

Type:Intership -  3 Months

Description: We are seeking a creative and talented Graphic Designer Intern to join our design team. This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain practical experience in graphic design, work on real projects, and contribute to our visual branding efforts. As a Graphic Designer Intern, you will collaborate with experienced designers and contribute to the creation of engaging and impactful visual materials.

Key Responsibilities::

  • Assist in the design and creation of various visual assets, including social media graphics, marketing materials, website elements, and more.
  • Collaborate with the design team to brainstorm and develop concepts for new projects.
  • Use graphic design software and tools to create visually appealing and effective designs.
  • Help maintain and update the visual branding guidelines to ensure consistency across all materials.
  • Support the design team in producing layouts, mockups, and prototypes for digital and print media.
  • Contribute to the development of creative ideas that align with project goals and target audiences.
  • Participate in design reviews and actively seek and provide constructive feedback.
  • Work on multiple projects simultaneously while adhering to deadlines and priorities.
  • Assist with photo editing, retouching, and image manipulation as needed.
  • Stay up-to-date with design trends and industry best practices to bring fresh ideas to the team.

    • Qualifications:

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Graphic Design, Visual Arts, or a related field (or recently graduated).
  • Proficiency in graphic design software such as Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, etc.).
  • A strong portfolio showcasing your design skills and creativity (include a link to your portfolio in your application).
  • Basic understanding of design principles, typography, color theory, and layout.
  • Creativity and the ability to think outside the box while adhering to brand guidelines.
  • Strong attention to detail and a commitment to delivering high-quality work.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate within a team environment.
  • Self-motivated and able to manage time effectively to meet project deadlines.
  • Enthusiasm for design trends and an eagerness to learn and grow as a designer.

    • Apply Now

    Why Join Sunrisemitra Global Technologies

    • Innovative Culture: Be part of a team that thrives on pushing boundaries.
    • Collaborative Environment: We value diverse perspectives and teamwork.
    • Professional Growth: Access learning opportunities and room to develop skills.
    • Impactful Work: Contribute to projects that make a difference.
    • Work-Life Balance: We value your well-being.

    1. Apply Online: Select the position and fill out the application form.
    2. Submission: Provide your details, resume, and cover letter.
    3. Review: Our team will review your application.
    4. Interview: If selected, you'll be contacted for an interview.
    5. Decision: We'll communicate our decision to you.

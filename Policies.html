Staff Welfare Measures

The institution is committed to the overall well-being of its staff. Various welfare measures are implemented including health care support, professional development opportunities, leave benefits, and financial assistance schemes. Recreational activities and recognition programs are also encouraged to create a positive and motivating work environment.

Policy Document on E-Governance

The college promotes transparency and efficiency through e-governance. Digital platforms are used for admissions, attendance, examinations, and administrative processes. Online communication, ERP systems, and e-documentation ensure accountability, accuracy, and quick decision-making while reducing paperwork.

Policy Document on Green Campus

The Green Campus policy reflects the institution’s commitment to sustainability and eco-friendly practices. Initiatives include energy conservation, waste management, tree plantation drives, use of renewable energy, and awareness programs. The policy aims to nurture a clean, green, and environmentally responsible campus.
